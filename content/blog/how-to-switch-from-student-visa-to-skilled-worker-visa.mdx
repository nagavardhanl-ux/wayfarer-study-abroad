You can switch from a UK Student visa to a Skilled Worker visa without leaving the UK. Here is when you can switch, what you need, and what changes after you switch.

## When you can switch

If you are in the UK on a Student visa, you can switch to a Skilled Worker visa if one of these applies:

- you have completed the course you were sponsored to study
- your job start date is after your course has finished
- you are studying a PhD full time, and have been for at least 24 months

<Source href="https://www.gov.uk/skilled-worker-visa/switch-to-this-visa" checked="2026-10-01" />

You can also switch from a Graduate visa.

## What you need

- **A job offer from a licensed sponsor**, with a certificate of sponsorship for that job.
- **A job at the right skill level and salary.** The general minimum is £41,700 a year, or the going rate for the job if that is higher.
- **English at CEFR level B2.** People who held a Skilled Worker visa before 8 January 2026 and are extending need level B1.

<Source href="https://www.gov.uk/skilled-worker-visa/your-job" checked="2026-10-01" />

## Lower salary for new entrants

You can be paid less if you are a new entrant. You count as a new entrant if:

- you are under 26 on the date you apply, or
- you are in the UK on a Student visa studying at bachelor's degree level or above, or you were in the last 2 years, or
- you are in the UK on a Graduate visa, or you were in the last 2 years.

New entrants must be paid at least £33,400 a year and 70% of the job's going rate. Your total stay on these new-entrant terms cannot be more than 4 years, including any time on a Graduate visa.

<Source href="https://www.gov.uk/skilled-worker-visa/when-you-can-be-paid-less" checked="2026-10-01" />

## Travelling while you switch

If you leave the UK after your course ends but before your Skilled Worker application is decided, you may face questions at the border on your return. It is safer to wait for the decision before travelling.

## Settling in the UK

Settlement (indefinite leave to remain) usually needs 5 years of living and working in the UK, for example on a Skilled Worker visa. Time on a Student visa does not count towards those 5 years.

<Source href="https://www.gov.uk/indefinite-leave-to-remain" checked="2026-10-01" />
