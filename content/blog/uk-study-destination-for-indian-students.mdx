Every year thousands of students from India choose the UK for higher studies. Choosing the right course, university and city is a big decision. These are the main reasons students pick the UK.

## One-year master's degrees

Many UK master's degrees take one year of full-time study. You pay one year of fees and one year of living costs, and you can start working sooner than on a two-year programme.

<Source href="https://study-uk.britishcouncil.org/moving-uk/cost-studying" checked="2026-10-01" />

## Work after you graduate

After a bachelor's or master's degree you can apply for the Graduate visa and stay in the UK to work or look for work. It lasts 2 years if you apply on or before 31 December 2026, and 18 months if you apply from 1 January 2027. PhD graduates get 3 years.

<Source href="https://www.gov.uk/graduate-visa" checked="2026-10-01" />

From the Graduate visa or after your course, you can switch to a Skilled Worker visa inside the UK if an employer sponsors you. Read [how to switch from a Student visa to a Skilled Worker visa](/how-to-switch-from-student-visa-to-skilled-worker-visa/).

## What it costs

International tuition in the UK ranges from £11,400 to £38,000 a year for undergraduate courses and £9,000 to £30,000 a year for postgraduate courses. See the costs in rupees, and the money you must show for the visa, on our [Study in the UK](/study-in-uk/) page.

## Industry links and placements

Many UK courses include internships or placement years, which let you gain work experience during your degree.

## Scholarships

The UK government's Chevening Scholarships, the GREAT Scholarships and the Commonwealth Scholarships are open to students from India. See them on our [scholarships page](/scholarships/).
