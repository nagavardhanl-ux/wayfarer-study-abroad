## What we do

We counsel students and their parents about studying abroad. We check each student's profile, shortlist universities, help with applications and SOPs, guide families on education loans, and prepare the student visa file. Students also take IELTS, PTE and other test coaching with us.

Our immigration team helps professionals check their eligibility for permanent residence in Canada, Australia and Germany. Our visit visa team helps families and tourists.

## How we work

- **Transparency.** Every fee is given in writing. We share application logins with students so they can see their own applications.
- **Honesty about outcomes.** Universities decide admissions and governments decide visas. We never promise either.
- **Legal and ethical standards.** We follow each country's rules and submit only true documents.
- **Parents in the room.** We hold sessions with students and parents together, because families make this decision together.

## Our aim

To help young people from South and West India reach the right university abroad, with clear advice on cost, loans and visas at every step.
