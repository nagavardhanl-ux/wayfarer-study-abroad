## Who we are

Wayfarer is an overseas education and visa consultancy with branches in Bengaluru, Chennai, Pune and Kochi. Wayfarer is a fictional company created for a portfolio project. In this policy, "we" means Wayfarer. For the purposes of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), we are the Data Fiduciary for the data you give us.

## What we collect

When you use the free profile check or contact us, we collect:

- your name, mobile number and email address
- your study plans: destination, course level, qualification and marks, English test status, budget range and nearest branch
- the page you submitted the form on, and the campaign details in the link you used to reach us (for example UTM tags)

When you browse the website, we may use Google Analytics to understand which pages are used. This uses cookies and collects device and usage information, not your name.

## Why we use it

We use your details only to:

- call, WhatsApp or email you about your study abroad plans
- assign your enquiry to the branch you chose
- improve our services and website

We process this data on the basis of your consent, which you give by ticking the consent box. We do not sell your data.

## Who we share it with

- **Our team** at the branch you choose.
- **Service providers** that store or process data for us, such as Google (Google Sheets and Google Analytics) and our website host. They may store data outside India.
- **Universities, lenders and visa services**, only when you ask us to apply on your behalf, and only what is needed.

## How long we keep it

We keep enquiry data for as long as we are helping you, and for up to 3 years after our last contact, unless the law requires longer. After that we delete it.

## Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can:

- ask what personal data we hold about you
- ask us to correct or update it
- withdraw your consent and ask us to delete your data
- raise a grievance with us, and then with the Data Protection Board of India

To use any of these rights, contact your nearest branch by phone or WhatsApp, or visit in person. We will reply within 30 days.

## Children

If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to fill in the form with you or for you.

## Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page with a new date.
